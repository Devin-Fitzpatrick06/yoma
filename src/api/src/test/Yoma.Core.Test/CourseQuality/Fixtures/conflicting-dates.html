Introduction to Data Analysis

About this course: the spring cohort.

Starts 2 March 2026. Ends 10 April 2026.

You will work through short practical exercises built around real datasets. Each unit ends with a quiz so you can check your progress, and our tutors answer questions in the discussion forum throughout. You will learn how to clean messy spreadsheets, summarise results and present your findings clearly to colleagues who are not specialists.
