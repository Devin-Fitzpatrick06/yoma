A full draft, with its sources shown

The AI fills the steps it can and leaves facts it can't know as questions for the partner.

4Every value is labelled by source. AI draft, from you, from your profile, or only you decide. The partner sees what to check without rereading everything.
5Additions are flagged, never hidden. The model added a CV clinic and an age range that weren't in the note. The partner keeps or removes each one. This is the main safeguard against made-up details.
6Missing facts become questions. Start time and step-free access are asked, not guessed (the shared data's missingInfo).
7Money and verification stay human. ZLTO, proof method and credentials are never drafted.

Why the pair matters

A drafting tool alone fills Yoma faster with more listings. Paired with Ziqi it fills Yoma with listings that are already verifiable. The two answers move the draft from Needs a look to Ready.
