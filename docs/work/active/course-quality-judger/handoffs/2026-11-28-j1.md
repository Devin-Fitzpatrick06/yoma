## Gotchas

- **The impact action is dated 29 November 2026** (the shared file's date). After that day its listing has ended and
  Ziqi says so; it is not on the click path. The event (12 December 2026) has the same limit later.
- **Course grades are relative to the catalogue.** On a quick-start database (40 graded courses) 002 to 004 can move a
  band; 001 and job 001 held A here with 2,015 graded.
- **Every assessment run regrades the whole catalogue** (`runGrading`), as the other start-up steps do.
- **The AI on a CPU model** sends the self-paced sample courses to review with a false "dates differ". `start.sh` runs
  the checks without the AI, so the demo is unaffected; "Run AI check" on one of them in front of an audience would
  show it.
- `journey.sh stop` kills each app's process tree; a SkillLens `next dev` started by hand is not its concern.
