## My Yoma (the lead's main focus)

A new tab next to Opportunities, at `/my-yoma`. First visit: 5 questions (goal, starting point, skills you already
have, hours a week, country). Then "Your world, your Yoma":

- **YoID card** at the top (purple): name, chips for starting point, country and hours, skills, opportunities
  completed, credentials, ZLTO, and a link to the existing `/yoid` page.
- **Goal banner**: courses, hours, weeks, progress.
- **Path**: 4 stages, 8–12 real Yoma courses. Each course shows the skill it adds, why it sits there, its Zeki grade
  chip and outcome chips.
- **Live jobs** in the chosen country, then **learner badges**.

Goals and their stages are configuration (`Pathway:Goals`), not code. There are 6 goals: digital marketer, sales and
customer service, web developer, data analyst, run my own business, health and care worker.

## Decisions worth keeping

- Outcomes never claim a cause or a multiplier; they show "N% went on to …" with n, and are hidden below 20 youth.
- Employment is unknown, not 0%, until Yoma has job completions. Outcome figures in the demo come from synthetic
  learners and are labelled as such.
- Drop-off flags a course for review and never lowers its grade.
- Badges are computed, not stored. Self-reported survey answers and one-click action-link completions never earn
  VerifiedSkill.
- "Better than the existing review" is said only if the paired agreement interval from the hand-rated sample sits
  above 0.
