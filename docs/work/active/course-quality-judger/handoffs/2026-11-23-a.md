# Handoff: jobs, part 2 (job grades)

Date: 2026-11-23. Branch: `feature/course-quality-judger`, on top of `5667e2fb`, with the uncommitted work of
`2026-11-20-a.md`. Nothing here is committed yet; the project lead approves commits.

## Decided

- Jobs get A–D grades like courses (project lead, 2026-11-23), ranked only against other jobs.
- A feed job's age is not checked (project lead, 2026-11-20; see the earlier handoff).

## Built

- **Job grade factors** (new `CourseGradeFactor` values, never in the course rubric), default weights in
  `CourseQualityJobGradingDefaults` (`CourseQuality:Jobs:Grading`, version `jobs-g1`):
  - PayTransparency 25: amount 100, a pay period or "market related" alone 30, nothing 0.
  - JobClarity 20: schedule or contract type, location, requirements, how to apply.
  - YouthFit 20: the experience-level field first; then years asked for (0: 100, 1: 90, 2: 70, 3: 50, 4: 35, 5+: 15);
    entry-level wording 95; months under a year 90; a school-leaving qualification only (matric, SSCE, WASSCE) 85;
    otherwise no data (neutral 50).
  - ListingSafety 15: status and the scam check's score.
  - PartnerReliability 10: the partner's verified share, smoothed (8 of 10 prior).
  - ApplicantFeedback 10: neutral until the "Did you apply?" survey exists.
- **Bands** (`CourseQualityJobGradingEngine`): the course bands (A 17%, B to 40%, C to 75%) with A needing a verified
  job and a stated pay amount. One difference from courses: below A a tied group takes the band of its middle rank,
  because feed jobs are templated and a 375-job tie otherwise fell past B and C into D (C was empty). A still takes a
  tied group only if it fits whole.
- **Facts shared by checks and grade:** `CourseQualityJobRules.Facts` reads pay, schedule, location, requirements,
  experience and education from custom fields first and the text otherwise.
- **Grading service:** with `Jobs:Enabled`, the regrade run also grades jobs (listings loaded with custom fields) in the
  same transaction. The course rubric page is unchanged: its distribution, factor rows and in-browser preview exclude
  jobs, and the course rubric still validates against course factors only. Browse ranking ignores job grades unless the
  new `Jobs:ShowInBrowse` is on.
- **Web:** `JobGradeFactor` type and labels, so a job's grade breakdown reads in the reviewer card. The browse tile is a
  course card (study time, cost, "View course details"), so it now renders only for Learning even if jobs carry a status.
