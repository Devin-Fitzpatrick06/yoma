# Handoff: Course Quality Judger iteration 2

Date: 2026-11-16. Branch: `feature/course-quality-judger`, on top of `e61486fc`. Nothing in this iteration is
committed yet; the project lead approves commits.

## Built

- **Course grades.** A grading engine scores eight factors (learner feedback, syllabus, provider reliability,
  comprehensiveness, length, completion, listing integrity, accessibility) and bands them by percentile: A "Top pick"
  best 17%, B 23%, C 35%, D the rest, with A floors. NotVerified and, by default, NeedsReview courses are not graded.
  Migration `20261116094914_ApplicationDb_CourseQuality_GradeAndFeedback` adds the Grade, GradeRubric and
  LearnerFeedback tables and two Assessment columns.
- **Editable rubric.** Defaults in `CourseQuality:Grading`; an admin override saved from `/admin/course-quality/rubric`,
  which regrades the whole catalogue in the browser as you edit and lists the courses that would change. A rubric is
  dry-run before it is saved.
- **Reviewer mode.** `/admin/course-quality/review`: one course at a time, why it is here, confidence against the
  threshold, each check, the grade breakdown, learner feedback, and what the learner and the partner see. Keyboard
  shortcuts V, N, S, arrows.
- **Seamless sides (demo only).** A Learner · Reviewer · Partner switcher behind `NEXT_PUBLIC_COURSE_QUALITY_DEMO`;
  the demo admin is also a partner admin, so switching is navigation.
- **"Did you take it?"** Signed-in learners who follow a course link are asked on a later visit (one day by default,
  one minute in the demo). Yes opens a two-step survey; answers feed the grade within 30 s. Comments stay with Yoma.
- **Every course.** The demo imports all 2,401 live Learning listings (public `api.yoma.world` search), assessed once
  against their live pages at the product's pace (about 40 min) and saved to `scripts/course-quality-demo/.cache/`
  (git-ignored), so later starts take about 2–3 minutes. Participation is scaled from live counts; learner feedback is
  synthetic and labelled so.

- **Reviewer mode, later the same day.** The card is three columns (why it's here with the checks, the grade
  preview, why it has no grade with learner feedback); the learner and partner previews were removed, and the "See as"
  links still open them. Verify and Not verified save in one click and the next course slides in; the dialog only opens
  when Verified needs a format. Back and forward navigation is gone, so the queue only moves forward. Notes can now be
  added only in that format dialog.

## Known gaps

- Partners see learner feedback only on held-back courses; the partner page lists fix notes only.
- Scores within Alison are bunched (most 71–74), so one point can move a band and large tie groups set the A share.
- Non-Alison courses are nearly all waiting for review (unreadable pages, bot walls), so they are not graded until a
  reviewer confirms them.
- `TreasuryReleaseDefaultsTests.MigrationIsDiscoveredAfterTheExistingTreasurySeed` fails because this branch's
  migrations come after the treasury one; it already failed after iteration 1.
- The demo build sometimes fails fetching Google Fonts; running `start.sh` again works.
